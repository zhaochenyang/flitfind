# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

*Last updated: September 28, 2026*

**FlitFind** (com.flitfind.app) is a local file-search utility. This policy describes how FlitFind handles data — in short: **it does not collect any.**

## English

**FlitFind collects no data. No data is collected, stored off-device, shared, sold, or transmitted anywhere — by us or by any third party.**

Specifically:

- **No network access.** The app has no network entitlement at all; it is technically incapable of connecting to the internet.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or telemetry of any kind.
- **All data stays on your Mac.** The search index and preferences are stored only in the app's local sandbox container on your machine.
- **File access only where you grant it.** FlitFind reads only the folders you explicitly pick via the system open panel. A security-scoped bookmark (stored locally) keeps that grant across launches. It never reads locations you have not authorized.
- **No account, no sign-in.** The app works fully offline with no registration.

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at
[github.com/zhaochenyang/flitfind/issues](https://github.com/zhaochenyang/flitfind/issues).

## 简体中文

**FlitFind 不收集任何数据。我们与任何第三方均不收集、不上传、不共享、不出售你的任何数据。**

具体说明:

- **无网络访问。** 应用不具备任何网络权限,在系统层面就无法联网。
- **无统计、无广告、无追踪。** 应用内不含任何统计 SDK、广告框架或遥测组件。
- **所有数据只留在你的 Mac 上。** 搜索索引与偏好设置仅保存在本机应用的沙盒容器中。
- **只读取你授权的文件夹。** FlitFind 仅读取你通过系统打开面板明确选择的文件夹(本地保存的安全作用域书签用于重启后保持授权),绝不读取未授权位置。
- **无需注册登录。** 应用完全离线工作。

如对本政策有疑问,请到
[github.com/zhaochenyang/flitfind/issues](https://github.com/zhaochenyang/flitfind/issues) 提交。
